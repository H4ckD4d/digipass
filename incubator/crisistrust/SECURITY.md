# Security Policy

**Project owner:** Chris Cruz | h4ckd4d

CrisisTrust is safety-sensitive software. Security reports should focus on vulnerabilities that could affect alert provenance, integrity, check-in privacy, community-resource quality, or availability.

## High-impact issues

Examples include:

- source-status spoofing;
- integrity-state bypass;
- unsafe rendering of imported data;
- hidden network exfiltration;
- unintended persistence of check-in data;
- privilege or trust escalation for community resources;
- stale-alert logic failures;
- accessibility regressions that hide critical instructions.

## Sensitive data

Do not include real crisis victim data, private messages, credentials, precise personal location histories, or other unnecessary personal data in public reports or fixtures.

Use synthetic examples whenever possible.

## Coordinated disclosure

Please avoid publishing exploitable details before maintainers have had a reasonable opportunity to investigate and fix the issue.

## Design boundary

CrisisTrust must not use security features as a pretext for hidden tracking or identity profiling.
