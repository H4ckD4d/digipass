    Family mode

    Consent-based digital safety

    Family mode is designed for shared safety planning, not hidden monitoring. Agree on what will be reviewed, minimize identifiers, avoid collecting private-message contents, and use platform safety/reporting features when concerns arise.

    ConsentReview only agreed accounts and settings.
    MinimizeKeep only what is necessary for protection.
    SupportUse trusted adults and appropriate reporting channels.
    ProtectPrioritize account hardening over investigation.

    Evidence minimization

    Preserve less, preserve better

    When a legitimate safety report needs documentation, record only the minimum useful context: date/time, platform, brief description, and a reference to where the original is retained securely. Do not put passwords, recovery codes, session tokens, precise live locations, or unnecessary private content into DSI files.
