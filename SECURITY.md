# Security and Responsible Use

> **Project owner and original creator: Chris Cruz | h4ckd4d**

h4ckd4d Digital Safety Intelligence is designed for self-protection, consensual family safety, education, and defensive security review.

## Non-negotiable safety boundaries

Do not use this project to:

- profile, locate, monitor, or track another person without a legitimate and lawful basis;
- collect passwords, recovery codes, session tokens, private-message contents, or authentication secrets;
- scrape or correlate third-party accounts for surveillance;
- expose personal information publicly;
- identify or locate minors from public data;
- impersonate another person;
- bypass account security, privacy settings, or platform restrictions;
- retaliate against, confront, or independently investigate a suspected harmful actor.

## Young people and family safety

When a young person is involved:

- minimize names, usernames, precise locations, school identifiers, and other sensitive details;
- do not publish evidence publicly;
- prefer trusted-adult support, platform reporting tools, and appropriate professional or lawful channels;
- avoid collecting private communications unless there is a legitimate need and proper authorization;
- prioritize immediate safety over attribution or investigation.

## Evidence handling

Only preserve what is necessary to document an incident. Prefer:

- timestamp;
- platform/service name;
- minimal description;
- a reference or screenshot where lawful and appropriate;
- actions already taken.

Do not store secrets or unrelated private material.

## Reporting project vulnerabilities

Do not include credentials, personal data, or sensitive third-party information in public GitHub issues. Report repository bugs using a minimal reproducible example with synthetic data.
