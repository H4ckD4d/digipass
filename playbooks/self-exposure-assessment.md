# Self-Exposure Assessment Playbook

> **Project owner: Chris Cruz | h4ckd4d**

This playbook helps a person review their own digital exposure without collecting third-party data.

## 1. Define scope

List only accounts, devices, domains, and profiles you own or administer.

## 2. Review account security

Check whether important accounts have:

- strong MFA;
- unique passwords;
- reviewed recovery methods;
- login/session alerts;
- no unknown active sessions.

Do not record passwords, recovery codes, or authentication tokens in the assessment.

## 3. Review public profile exposure

Ask:

- Is the profile more public than necessary?
- Are phone numbers, addresses, school/work details, or routine/location information exposed unnecessarily?
- Are old posts or profile fields still useful to keep public?

Record the category and risk level, not unnecessary raw personal data.

## 4. Review app and device permissions

Check whether apps still need access to:

- location;
- contacts;
- camera/microphone;
- photos/files;
- background activity.

Remove permissions or applications that are no longer needed.

## 5. Review recovery paths

Confirm recovery email/phone methods are current and protected. Where available, use secure recovery options and store recovery codes privately rather than in the DSI assessment.

## 6. Prioritize findings

Use the DSI dimensions:

- exposure;
- impact;
- control weakness;
- confidence;
- urgency.

## 7. Take protective action

Focus on the highest-scoring items first. Re-run the assessment after changes to measure improvement.
