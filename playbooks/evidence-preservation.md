# Minimal Evidence Preservation Playbook

> **Project owner: Chris Cruz | h4ckd4d**

This playbook helps document a concerning digital-safety event without turning the user into an investigator or collecting unnecessary private data.

## Goal

Preserve enough context to support a legitimate report or trusted review while minimizing sensitive content.

## Recommended minimal record

Record only what is necessary, such as:

- date/time;
- service or platform;
- short factual summary;
- reference to an existing screenshot or report identifier where appropriate;
- action already taken, such as block, mute, security review, or platform report.

Use `schemas/evidence.schema.json` as the structured model.

## Avoid collecting

Do not place these in the evidence record:

- passwords;
- authentication tokens;
- recovery codes;
- unrelated private conversations;
- precise live location;
- large exports of another person's profile;
- sensitive information unrelated to the event.

## Preserve, do not escalate

If a record is needed:

1. capture the minimum relevant information;
2. do not alter or fabricate the material;
3. keep it private;
4. do not publish it to identify or shame another person;
5. use trusted, platform, professional, or lawful reporting channels as appropriate.

## Young people

When a young person is involved, minimize identifying details further and involve a trusted adult when the situation is persistent, threatening, or difficult to handle alone.

The goal is **safety and support**, not independent attribution or confrontation.
